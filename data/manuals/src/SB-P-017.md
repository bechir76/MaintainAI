# Service Bulletin SB-P-017: Mechanical Seal Upgrade for P-100/250

## 1. Summary

Pumps of type P-100/250 built before 2023 were delivered with a carbon / silicon carbide mechanical seal with EPDM elastomers. On sites with liquid temperatures close to 90 °C, early seal wear has been reported after 6,000 to 9,000 operating hours.

An upgraded seal with silicon carbide / silicon carbide faces and FKM elastomers is now available as kit **SK-250-SIC**. The upgraded seal is recommended at the next scheduled seal replacement.

## 2. Affected equipment

- All P-100/250 pumps with serial numbers below 23-0000.
- P-80/200 pumps are not affected.

## 3. Instructions

1. Order kit SK-250-SIC for each affected pump.
2. Replace the seal following P-MT-02 section 5. The fitting dimensions are unchanged.
3. Use only water or the seal lubricant from the kit on FKM elastomers.
4. Record the seal type change in the maintenance ticket and on the pump record card.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Technical notes

| Item | Original seal | Upgraded seal SK-250-SIC |
|---|---|---|
| Faces | Carbon / SiC | SiC / SiC |
| Elastomers | EPDM | FKM |
| Max liquid temperature | 90 °C | 120 °C |
| Expected life at 85 °C | about 8,000 h | about 16,000 h |

The upgraded seal is more sensitive to dry running than the carbon seal. Always fill and vent the pump completely before start-up.
